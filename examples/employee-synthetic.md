# Employee example: payroll and work-tool barriers

**Entirely synthetic.** Organisation, people, findings and decisions below are fictional. No real participant, tool execution, approval, supplier or outcome is implied. All actions and benefits are proposals until a real adoption and evaluation.

## Profile and remit

A small fictional employer uses a purchased payroll service and several collaboration tools. Staff report that an essential payroll activity is inaccessible and that repeated cross-tool authentication adds burden. No dedicated accessibility team exists. Actual jurisdiction, legal applicability, named decision owners, budget and individual support needs are unknown.

The proposed remit is employee services, initially the payroll task and its authentication/support dependencies. The intended outcome is independent access to essential payroll information with understandable recovery and sustainable effort. Role labels below are proposals for local assignment.

## Decision exercise

If the employer has no confirmed execution mandate, the assistant can draft the programme, supplier questions and decision brief at A1. It cannot send them, change payroll or access individual records merely because the example mentions those actions. Confirm actual permissions before A2/A3.

If manual/task evidence later fails while an automated scan passes, keep the failure under R05, return to P06/P07 and block the unsupported claim. An authorised human makes any permissible transition decision with the remaining defect visible. An exception does not change the failed result or waive an obligation.

## Proposed reporting and next decisions

Team view: affected task/configuration, known unknowns, supplier dependencies, proposed correction and retest owner. Leadership view: impact on essential work, interim access quality, options, funding/authority gaps and decision consequences.

Proposed outcome measures are access to the critical task, independence/control, cumulative effort, confidence, support quality and meaningful resolution. Actual baseline/current values are **not assessed**. Agree sample, disclosure controls and evidence before collection. Keep adjustment/health/performance records outside the shared programme.

Next: confirm actual service owner and support route; verify relevant facts and source baselines; commission proportionate evidence; approve feasible work. This exercise is a documented process illustration, not evidence of operational effectiveness.
