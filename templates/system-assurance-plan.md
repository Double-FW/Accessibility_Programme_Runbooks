## Traceability and change

Record stable IDs/revisions and many-to-many relationships among brand purposes, user outcomes, foundations, tokens, patterns/components, requirements, techniques, methodologies, reference implementations, tests/scripts, evidence and consuming services. Preserve source provenance, owners, raw results and known limitations. Trace changed assets to consumers for regression and outcome review; do not silently inherit a pass.

Plan criteria and missing assets early, then reference actual evaluated assets/results before relying on them in an affected decision. This template does not invent reference implementations or require all documents to exist before unrelated work or urgent support proceeds.
