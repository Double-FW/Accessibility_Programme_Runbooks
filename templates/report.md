# Accessibility programme report

**Template — adapt for team, programme, leadership or participant audience.**

## Report context

Record report ID/version, author/reviewer, audience, decision purpose, period, scope, runbooks, source baseline and secure evidence references. State whether this is a draft or an authorised communication.

## What changed for people

Describe changes in understanding, control, participation, task outcome, independence, effort, confidence/satisfaction and support quality. Give scope and evidence; distinguish observed change from inference. State whose voices are missing and what changed because of participation.

| Measure/outcome | Scope and period | Baseline/current | Denominator and coverage | Missingness/uncertainty | Evidence | Owner/next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Locally agreed measure. | Activity/context. | Actual values or not assessed. | Relevant denominator and method. | Preserve limits. | Secure references. | Named owner and action. |

## Evidence and remaining barriers

Keep authority/commitments, capability, technical delivery and human experience separate. List material failures, unassessed scope, adverse findings, supplier constraints, source changes, interim access and corrective/retest plans. Include subgroup disparities without exposing individuals or overclaiming prevalence.

## Decisions required

| Decision | Why now and impact | Options and consequences | Evidence/gaps | Actual owner | Required by / review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local decision ID. | People and service effect. | Include deferral impact. | References and uncertainty. | Confirm authority. | Locally agreed or proposed. |

## Tailor the view

- Teams need reproducible findings, dependencies, work owners and retest/handoffs.
- Programme managers need cross-workstream outcomes, capacity and overdue decisions.
- Senior management need material exclusion, commitments at risk, options and investment decisions.
- Affected people need understandable changes, limitations and usable support/feedback routes.
- External claims need current applicable formats, substantiation and actual authorisation.

## AI contribution and learning

Record actual AI use, error/correction rates, verification burden, time released, missed findings and participant effects. Do not equate generated output volume with benefit. Record learning, changes to delegation and the next outcome review.

Before distribution, check material claims against evidence, disclosure permission, accessibility of the delivered format and actual review status. Use [reporting guidance](../framework/reporting-and-learning.md) and relevant [production sources](../sources/README.md#s21).

## System and brand evidence

Report foundation/practice and component/pattern coverage, missing reference implementations/scripts, adoption, configured consumers and regression impacts. Distinguish document coverage, actual conformance, accessible usability and brand identification/trust/expectations/value/values/resonance. Reference the [system-assurance plan](system-assurance-plan.md) and preserve scope/limits.
