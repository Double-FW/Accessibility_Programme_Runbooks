# Programme plan

**Template — proposed until actual local decisions are recorded.**

## Remit and intended outcomes

Record plan ID/version, profile reference, selected runbooks, sponsor, responsible coordinator, scope, jurisdictions, critical journeys, affected contexts and exclusions with reasons. State intended participation outcomes and the next material decision. Separate confirmed facts, hypotheses and proposed targets.

Prioritise blocked essential tasks, harm, applicable obligations, repeated/cumulative burden and systemic dependencies, informed by affected people. Explain consequences of deferral and interim access. ROI hypotheses can inform choices but absence of a commercial forecast does not justify denying access.

## Participation, evidence and service recovery

Record accessible participation throughout framing, prioritisation, design and evaluation; what influence will be documented; intended evidence methods and sample limits; secure data arrangements; immediate support/recovery; and permanent correction/retest owners.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use [decision/evidence records](decision-and-evidence.md). Include supplier, source, model, policy, organisation and audience changes as review triggers. Distinguish workflow completion, assessment outcome and authorised approval. Do not prescribe universal dates, thresholds or staffing ratios.

## Benefit and reporting plan

Name each reporting audience and decision, selected measures, baseline, period, denominator, missingness and limitations. Compare AI contribution with verification burden, correction rate, time released and actual participation outcomes. Refer to the [report template](report.md).

## UX design, branding and QA capability workstream

Use the [system-assurance plan](system-assurance-plan.md) to plan accessible UX and branding systems explicitly, with owners, confirmed/proposed capacity and milestones. Require guidance/manual/automatable coverage for every in-scope foundation; design requirements, techniques, methodologies, reference implementations and user ACT-style scripts for patterns/components; and evidence for the six brand purposes without sacrificing identity. Detailed assets remain in owning systems. Record gaps, dependencies and actual outcomes separately from authored-document status.
