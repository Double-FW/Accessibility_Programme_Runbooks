# Employee-facing services and systems

**Status:** draft for local adoption. Use this runbook to design and operate a programme; it is not an organisation-specific approved plan.

Enable people to obtain work, perform meaningful activities, collaborate, learn, progress and leave with equitable access, autonomy and dignity. Examine the whole system of work: tools, content, policies, environments and support. A single accessible application can coexist with a disabling workflow or unsustainable cumulative burden. Include candidates, contractors and outsourced services within the adopted remit.

## Start here

1. Record one critical activity, scope, known barriers and actual owner in the [organisation profile](../templates/organisation-profile.md). Use progressive intake; do not wait for perfect inventory to address known serious exclusion.
2. Confirm relevant obligations and adopted commitments through P02; choose appropriate source modules from the [catalogue](../sources/README.md).
3. Follow the steps below with the [shared P01–P09 cycle](../framework/programme-cycle.md), [routing](../framework/decision-routing.md) and [human/AI responsibilities](../framework/human-ai-responsibilities.md).
4. Build an owned [programme plan](../templates/programme-plan.md) and preserve source/evidence/decision links through the [record template](../templates/decision-and-evidence.md).
5. Review actual outcomes and support needs, then report and replan. Missing authority/evidence blocks the affected decision while independent work continues.

## Accessible UX and branding systems

Include accessible UX design and branding systems as owned programme capabilities. Plan adoption, creation, remediation or extension; verify shared/supplier assets instead of assuming reuse proves accessibility. Require foundation/practice and pattern/component assurance as defined in the [system process](../framework/design-and-brand-systems.md), using its [plan](../templates/system-assurance-plan.md). This includes guidance, manual/automatable coverage, design and technical references, methodologies, reference implementations and user ACT-style scripts. Preserve the six brand purposes and evaluate accessible usability and communication alongside conformance. Detailed system content remains in its owning sources.

## Runbook steps

These audience-specific steps supplement the shared process. The P identifiers name the management/delivery stages to apply; they are not a second mandatory linear lifecycle. For each adopted activity, confirm one human accountable owner and a responsible human, even where AI executes bounded work.

### E01 — Map work and dependencies

**Trigger:** Programme intake, role/workflow change or repeated barrier. **Shared stages:** P01, P03.

**Prerequisites:** Available task observations and approved research access.

1. Map representative role activities and complete tasks across systems, communication, content and environments.
2. Record frequency, interruptions, handoffs, interaction methods and cumulative burden across a working day.

**Human owner:** Service/work-design owner with workers and research lead. **AI support:** Structure authorised observations into task and dependency maps; propose gaps to investigate.

**Evidence/handoff:** Task map with actual contexts and scope; Participation record and unknown activities.

**Exit:** Affected workers and research/service owners have reviewed the map or gaps remain explicit. **Next:** E02; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S11](../sources/README.md#s11), [S12](../sources/README.md#s12), [S16](../sources/README.md#s16), [S17](../sources/README.md#s17), [S18](../sources/README.md#s18), [S19](../sources/README.md#s19).


**System assurance:** Include the UX design and branding systems governing employee tools, internal communications and work activities. Use the [design/brand system process](../framework/design-and-brand-systems.md) and [assurance plan](../templates/system-assurance-plan.md); retain relevant evidence and open gaps in this handoff.

### E02 — Route systemic barriers and confidential support

**Trigger:** Individual request, system failure or work-impact concern. **Shared stages:** P03, P09.

**Prerequisites:** Minimum necessary barrier description and actual receiving owners.

1. Route common service defects to the system/process owner.
2. Use the confidential adjustment/support route for individual needs; retain linkage through restricted references and disclosure choice.

**Human owner:** Service owner for defects; authorised support/HR owner for confidential case within separate remits. **AI support:** Prepare minimal handoffs within data permission; never determine adjustment entitlement or employment outcomes.

**Evidence/handoff:** Systemic issue and separate restricted support reference if relevant; Receiving owners and immediate access action.

**Exit:** Both routes have owners where needed; no confidential detail is exposed in the shared backlog. **Next:** E03; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S11](../sources/README.md#s11), [S19](../sources/README.md#s19), [S20](../sources/README.md#s20).

### E03 — Establish a qualified employee baseline

**Trigger:** Evidence needed for priority, supplier choice or outcome evaluation. **Shared stages:** P02, P03, P07.

**Prerequisites:** Task scope, relevant requirements and approved evaluation methods.

1. Combine technical, manual/AT, comparative-task and human-experience evidence without merging their meanings.
2. Record task settings, support dependence, sustainable effort, error/recovery and cumulative effects; separate observation from inference.

**Human owner:** Evaluation/research owner with affected workers and competent specialists. **AI support:** Analyse approved evidence and flag missingness; do not rank workers, infer competence or treat fastest completion as the goal.

**Evidence/handoff:** Baseline with methods, environments, dates and limitations; Requirement and task-outcome records with source provenance.

**Exit:** Evidence supports scoped prioritisation or the missing evidence has an owner. **Next:** E04; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S08](../sources/README.md#s08), [S11](../sources/README.md#s11), [S12](../sources/README.md#s12), [S13](../sources/README.md#s13), [S14](../sources/README.md#s14), [S16](../sources/README.md#s16).

### E04 — Fund systemic improvement and interim access

**Trigger:** Priorities, support options, procurement or renewal decisions. **Shared stages:** P04, P05.

**Prerequisites:** Qualified impact evidence, owner and proposed options.

1. Prioritise essential blocked work, significant harm, repeated burden and dependencies.
2. Agree practical interim access, supplier actions and permanent correction; consider who bears extra effort and preserve privacy and choice.

**Human owner:** Sponsor/service owner within delegation with procurement and confidential support owner as appropriate. **AI support:** Compare options, dependencies and consequences of deferral; keep funding and authority proposals unconfirmed.

**Evidence/handoff:** Owned workstream and resource decisions; Interim support quality and review conditions.

**Exit:** Approved work is feasible and owned; unfunded gaps and unresolved harms remain visible. **Next:** E05; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S01](../sources/README.md#s01), [S02](../sources/README.md#s02), [S06](../sources/README.md#s06), [S07](../sources/README.md#s07), [S11](../sources/README.md#s11), [S12](../sources/README.md#s12), [S17](../sources/README.md#s17), [S18](../sources/README.md#s18), [S32](../sources/README.md#s32), [S33](../sources/README.md#s33).


**System assurance:** Plan owned design-system and branding-system capability work with manual and automatable QA coverage and employee usability outcomes. Use the [design/brand system process](../framework/design-and-brand-systems.md) and [assurance plan](../templates/system-assurance-plan.md); retain relevant evidence and open gaps in this handoff.

### E05 — Deliver, evaluate and transition

**Trigger:** Service, tool, workflow, content or supplier change. **Shared stages:** P05, P06, P07, P08.

**Prerequisites:** Bounded brief, source baseline, evidence plan and relevant permissions.

1. Use the shared delivery cycle for integrations, authentication, work tools, learning and communications.
2. Evaluate realistic complete work activities and exact delivered assets; retain failures and retest after correction.

**Human owner:** Delivery and evaluation owners; authorised service owner makes transition decision. **AI support:** Prepare reviewable assets, run supported checks and assemble evidence within delegation.

**Evidence/handoff:** Versioned deliverables and actual evaluation results; Authenticated transition decision and operational handoff.

**Exit:** Relevant P08 decision recorded with support, open issues, correction and review conditions. **Next:** E06; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S13](../sources/README.md#s13), [S14](../sources/README.md#s14), [S16](../sources/README.md#s16), [S17](../sources/README.md#s17), [S18](../sources/README.md#s18), [S20](../sources/README.md#s20), [S21](../sources/README.md#s21), [S22](../sources/README.md#s22), [S23](../sources/README.md#s23), [S24](../sources/README.md#s24), [S25](../sources/README.md#s25), [S27](../sources/README.md#s27), [S32](../sources/README.md#s32), [S33](../sources/README.md#s33).


**System assurance:** Use the system-assurance plan for foundations/components, versioned reference implementations and user ACT-style scripts; preserve internal brand purpose. Use the [design/brand system process](../framework/design-and-brand-systems.md) and [assurance plan](../templates/system-assurance-plan.md); retain relevant evidence and open gaps in this handoff.

### E06 — Sustain participation and learn

**Trigger:** Operation, feedback, role/tool change, redesign or absence/return. **Shared stages:** P09.

**Prerequisites:** Operational owner and accessible confidential feedback routes.

1. Check whether support and correction deliver meaningful work participation over time.
2. Review cumulative barriers after change; feed learning into procurement, design, training and funded improvement.

**Human owner:** Operational/workplace owner with programme and confidential support owners. **AI support:** Prompt agreed reviews and summarise permitted aggregates; no surveillance or individual productivity scoring.

**Evidence/handoff:** Outcome review and owned corrective actions; Restricted case details kept separate from shared programme reporting.

**Exit:** Next actions and review triggers known; ongoing service remains monitored within permission. **Next:** E01; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S01](../sources/README.md#s01), [S06](../sources/README.md#s06), [S07](../sources/README.md#s07), [S11](../sources/README.md#s11), [S12](../sources/README.md#s12), [S19](../sources/README.md#s19).

## Decision branches

- **Essential work blocked:** R01 for immediate agreed support, with permanent correction through E04/E05. Do not delay help until a complete programme assessment.
- **System barrier and individual need coexist:** R04 routes service correction and restricted support in parallel. An adjustment does not prove a systemic defect is fixed.
- **Supplier controls the tool:** R03 requires evaluation of the actual configured task and integration; compare remediation, renewal conditions, support, alternatives and replacement.
- **Performance/absence may reflect inaccessible work:** route to the authorised fair-review process under R04. Preserve barrier evidence separately from employment judgements.
- **AT or meeting/AI tool transmits information:** R08 reviews actual data flows and third-party information, while preserving access during review.
- **Evidence covers only some roles/settings:** retain the remaining scope as unassessed; R05 blocks broader conclusions.

## Reporting outcomes

Track participation in critical activities, cumulative effort, independence/control, confidence and satisfaction, support quality and time to meaningful resolution. Explain systemic barriers and supplier dependencies. Keep case-level health, adjustments, applicant, performance and grievance data in approved confidential systems; avoid recognisable small-group reporting. Never turn comparative task analysis into employee productivity ranking.

Use the [report template](../templates/report.md). Separate authority, capability, technical delivery and human experience. Give scope, period, denominator, missingness and uncertainty; preserve failed requirements alongside satisfaction.

## Use with AI

Use the [programme-design prompt](../prompts/design-programme.md). Select A0–A3 per task; require actual execution permission and evidence. AI may support research planning, option analysis, drafts, checks and reporting. It must not infer disability, fabricate participants, authenticate its own approval or make rights-affecting decisions. Preserve an accessible human route.
