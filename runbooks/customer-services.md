# Customer-facing services and systems

**Status:** draft for local adoption. Use this runbook to design and operate a programme; it is not an organisation-specific approved plan.

Enable customers and service users to discover, understand, choose, obtain, use, obtain support for, change and leave a service with equivalent experience, meaningful choice and satisfaction. Include non-purchasing public-service users and relevant intermediaries. Join brand promises, service behaviour and operational delivery across channels. This lifecycle is the framework's proposed process structure; it does not claim a dedicated customer-lifecycle source repository.

## Start here

1. Record one critical activity, scope, known barriers and actual owner in the [organisation profile](../templates/organisation-profile.md). Use progressive intake; do not wait for perfect inventory to address known serious exclusion.
2. Confirm relevant obligations and adopted commitments through P02; choose appropriate source modules from the [catalogue](../sources/README.md).
3. Follow the steps below with the [shared P01–P09 cycle](../framework/programme-cycle.md), [routing](../framework/decision-routing.md) and [human/AI responsibilities](../framework/human-ai-responsibilities.md).
4. Build an owned [programme plan](../templates/programme-plan.md) and preserve source/evidence/decision links through the [record template](../templates/decision-and-evidence.md).
5. Review actual outcomes and support needs, then report and replan. Missing authority/evidence blocks the affected decision while independent work continues.

## Accessible UX and branding systems

Include accessible UX design and branding systems as owned programme capabilities. Plan adoption, creation, remediation or extension; verify shared/supplier assets instead of assuming reuse proves accessibility. Require foundation/practice and pattern/component assurance as defined in the [system process](../framework/design-and-brand-systems.md), using its [plan](../templates/system-assurance-plan.md). This includes guidance, manual/automatable coverage, design and technical references, methodologies, reference implementations and user ACT-style scripts. Preserve the six brand purposes and evaluate accessible usability and communication alongside conformance. Detailed system content remains in its owning sources.

## Lifecycle coverage

| Lifecycle area | Include in scope where relevant |
| --- | --- |
| Discover, understand and choose | Advertising, brand, information, comparison, eligibility, language and consequences. |
| Register and obtain | Application, identity, authentication, purchase/payment or enrolment, confirmations and onboarding. |
| Use and receive | Service use, fulfilment, account management, documents, media and physical handoffs. |
| Support and change | Telephone, chat, assisted channels, recovery, complaints, redress, renewals and changed circumstances. |
| Leave and close | Cancellation, exit, data requests and clear confirmation without coercive barriers. |

## Runbook steps

These audience-specific steps supplement the shared process. The P identifiers name the management/delivery stages to apply; they are not a second mandatory linear lifecycle. For each adopted activity, confirm one human accountable owner and a responsible human, even where AI executes bounded work.

### C01 — Map the complete customer journey

**Trigger:** New service, major change, complaint or journey gap. **Shared stages:** P01, P03.

**Prerequisites:** Service purpose, channels and available customer evidence.

1. Map discovery, understanding, choice, transaction/enrolment, use, support, change, complaint and exit.
2. Record promises, decisions, intermediaries, recovery and handoffs across digital, assisted and physical channels.

**Human owner:** Service/CX owner with research and customer participants. **AI support:** Draft journey maps from approved evidence and identify assumptions; customer research validates them.

**Evidence/handoff:** End-to-end journey/dependency map; Participation and unobserved contexts.

**Exit:** Scope covers relevant failure, support and exit paths; gaps remain explicit. **Next:** C02; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S04](../sources/README.md#s04), [S05](../sources/README.md#s05), [S17](../sources/README.md#s17), [S18](../sources/README.md#s18), [S19](../sources/README.md#s19).


**System assurance:** Identify the UX design and branding systems connecting communication promises, components and the whole customer journey. Use the [design/brand system process](../framework/design-and-brand-systems.md) and [assurance plan](../templates/system-assurance-plan.md); retain relevant evidence and open gaps in this handoff.

### C02 — Identify contexts and disabling demands

**Trigger:** Uncertain audience, complexity, urgency or changing capacity. **Shared stages:** P02, P03.

**Prerequisites:** Scoped journey and approved discovery methods.

1. Examine sensory, motor, cognitive, communication and intersecting needs plus language, environment and time-varying capacity.
2. Consider complexity, emergency or emotionally consequential decisions, ageing and service/product effects; commission appropriate expertise when needed.

**Human owner:** Research/service owner with affected people and relevant specialists. **AI support:** Generate hypotheses and evaluation questions without diagnosing, profiling or inferring vulnerability from behaviour.

**Evidence/handoff:** Barrier/context hypotheses distinguished from observed evidence; Requirements/applicability review for relevant service and jurisdiction.

**Exit:** Risks and evidence gaps routed to owners without unsupported personal labels. **Next:** C03; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S08](../sources/README.md#s08), [S09](../sources/README.md#s09), [S10](../sources/README.md#s10), [S16](../sources/README.md#s16), [S19](../sources/README.md#s19), [S20](../sources/README.md#s20).

### C03 — Define equivalent outcomes and measures

**Trigger:** Baseline or outcome criteria needed. **Shared stages:** P03, P04.

**Prerequisites:** Journey scope, ethical research plan and decision purpose.

1. Define understanding, control, task success, effort, privacy, confidence and satisfaction across channels.
2. If using functional-needs segmentation, including a scoped EAA lens, retrieve owning guidance and approve data collection, sampling and interpretation.

**Human owner:** Research/data owner with service, privacy and participant input. **AI support:** Draft measure specifications and analyse authorised evidence; distinguish sample limits and hypotheses.

**Evidence/handoff:** Outcome/evidence plan with baseline, coverage and missingness; Data governance and participant influence record.

**Exit:** Measures support a real decision and avoid compulsory diagnostic labels or unsupported population claims. **Next:** C04; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S09](../sources/README.md#s09), [S16](../sources/README.md#s16), [S17](../sources/README.md#s17), [S19](../sources/README.md#s19), [S30](../sources/README.md#s30).

### C04 — Prioritise and design meaningful alternatives

**Trigger:** Funding, design, supplier or channel decisions. **Shared stages:** P04, P05.

**Prerequisites:** Qualified evidence, requirement routes and feasible options.

1. Prioritise serious exclusion and harm; compare policy, process, service, content and interface interventions.
2. Examine marketing promises, support, retention, cancellation and complaint access; assess assisted alternatives for choice, privacy, cost, delay and outcome.

**Human owner:** Sponsor/service owner with design, procurement and operations. **AI support:** Compare alternatives and dependencies while leaving funding, legal and material risk decisions to authorised humans.

**Evidence/handoff:** Owned roadmap and option appraisal; Alternative-channel outcome and acceptance plan.

**Exit:** Affected work funded/owned with clear unresolved decisions; a support channel is not assumed equivalent. **Next:** C05; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S01](../sources/README.md#s01), [S02](../sources/README.md#s02), [S05](../sources/README.md#s05), [S17](../sources/README.md#s17), [S18](../sources/README.md#s18), [S19](../sources/README.md#s19), [S27](../sources/README.md#s27), [S32](../sources/README.md#s32), [S33](../sources/README.md#s33).


**System assurance:** Plan accessible UX and branding systems with traceable QA, preserving identification, trust, expectations, value, values and resonance. Use the [design/brand system process](../framework/design-and-brand-systems.md) and [assurance plan](../templates/system-assurance-plan.md); retain relevant evidence and open gaps in this handoff.

### C05 — Deliver and evaluate complete journeys

**Trigger:** Implementation, production, service configuration or release. **Shared stages:** P05, P06, P07, P08.

**Prerequisites:** Brief, source baseline, exact assets/builds and permissions.

1. Integrate accessible content/media, operational staff, suppliers and interaction channels from the brief.
2. Evaluate authentication, state changes, transactions, errors, handoffs, support and exit in actual configurations; correct and retest.

**Human owner:** Delivery/evaluation owners; authorised service/release owner decides. **AI support:** Draft and review code/content, run supported authorised checks and assemble evidence without substituting synthetic users.

**Evidence/handoff:** Actual technical, editorial and task/research findings with limits; Release/transition decision and receiving operational owner.

**Exit:** P08 decision has adequate evidence and known open issues, support and recovery arrangements. **Next:** C06; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S13](../sources/README.md#s13), [S14](../sources/README.md#s14), [S16](../sources/README.md#s16), [S17](../sources/README.md#s17), [S18](../sources/README.md#s18), [S21](../sources/README.md#s21), [S22](../sources/README.md#s22), [S23](../sources/README.md#s23), [S24](../sources/README.md#s24), [S25](../sources/README.md#s25), [S26](../sources/README.md#s26), [S27](../sources/README.md#s27), [S28](../sources/README.md#s28), [S29](../sources/README.md#s29), [S31](../sources/README.md#s31), [S32](../sources/README.md#s32), [S33](../sources/README.md#s33).


**System assurance:** Evaluate linked foundations, components, reference implementations and user ACT-style scripts, plus brand outcomes beyond technical conformance. Use the [design/brand system process](../framework/design-and-brand-systems.md) and [assurance plan](../templates/system-assurance-plan.md); retain relevant evidence and open gaps in this handoff.

### C06 — Operate, recover and improve

**Trigger:** Live feedback, incident, complaint, source change or agreed review. **Shared stages:** P09.

**Prerequisites:** Operational owner, usable support and permitted monitoring.

1. Combine complaints, support, research and observed failures; verify correction in the delivered service.
2. Preserve subgroup disparities, recovery burdens and unresolved barriers; review whether participation changed decisions.

**Human owner:** Operational/service owner with programme, research and reporting owners. **AI support:** Analyse permitted aggregates and propose actions; do not infer disability or causation from abandonment.

**Evidence/handoff:** Outcome review and claim-to-evidence report; Owned corrections, source impact assessments and review triggers.

**Exit:** Actions and next decisions owned; continuous operation loops to affected earlier steps. **Next:** C01; return to the affected shared stage if prerequisites or conclusions change.

**Sources:** [S01](../sources/README.md#s01), [S06](../sources/README.md#s06), [S17](../sources/README.md#s17), [S19](../sources/README.md#s19), [S20](../sources/README.md#s20), [S30](../sources/README.md#s30).

## Decision branches

- **Time-critical or consequential activity blocked:** R01 activates recovery and escalation. Record delay, cost, disclosure and dependence introduced by interim access.
- **Assisted access proposed:** R11 evaluates information, control, privacy, availability and outcome. A telephone number or chatbot alone does not establish equivalence.
- **Channel or organisation handoff:** preserve context and consent, avoid unnecessary repetition and verify end-to-end completion under R11.
- **Complexity, urgency, emotional stress or changing capability:** C02 and S16 inform research/design; specialist review is needed for unsupported clinical or trauma claims. Consider the disabling effects of the service/product itself, including consumption-related effects where relevant, without profiling individuals.
- **Functional-needs segmentation:** C03 uses governed research and owning guidance, not diagnosis or compulsory customer labels. Scope any EAA lens independently from legal applicability.
- **Commercial metrics rise while access worsens:** R09 preserves disparities and failed requirements; aggregate conversion or satisfaction cannot erase exclusion.
- **Content, video, chat, immersive or game service:** R12 selects the corresponding source modules at briefing, not only final QA.

## Reporting outcomes

Track complete-journey success, comprehension, independence, control, effort, confidence/satisfaction, recovery, support dependence, complaints/redress and exit. Separate observed facts from hypotheses about abandonment. Report relevant functional contexts with sampling and disclosure limits; do not infer diagnosis, disability or vulnerability from telemetry.

Use the [report template](../templates/report.md). Separate authority, capability, technical delivery and human experience. Give scope, period, denominator, missingness and uncertainty; preserve failed requirements alongside satisfaction.

## Use with AI

Use the [programme-design prompt](../prompts/design-programme.md). Select A0–A3 per task; require actual execution permission and evidence. AI may support research planning, option analysis, drafts, checks and reporting. It must not infer disability, fabricate participants, authenticate its own approval or make rights-affecting decisions. Preserve an accessible human route.
