# Reporting and learning

Use [S01](../sources/README.md#s01) for programme reporting, [S06](../sources/README.md#s06) for capability, [S19](../sources/README.md#s19) for data ethics and [S30](../sources/README.md#s30) for strategic/ESG interpretation. Reports are decision tools, not an undifferentiated accessibility score.

## Keep evidence lenses distinct

| Lens | Useful question | Does not establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authority and commitments | Who agreed what for which scope? | Implementation or achieved outcomes. |
| Organisational capability | Are roles, capacity, skills and controls working? | Equivalent experience for each person or task. |
| Delivery and technical evaluation | What behaviour was implemented and evaluated? | Complete human experience or legal compliance. |
| Human experience | Can people understand, control and achieve the activity with dignity and sustainable effort? | Population prevalence or causation without suitable evidence. |

## Produce views from one evidence base

| Audience | Include | Decision required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery teams | Scope, actual failures, reproducible evidence, dependencies, owners and retest. | What to correct, investigate or hand over next. |
| Programme management | Outcomes by workstream, capacity, supplier dependencies, overdue decisions and source changes. | Replan, unblock, escalate or commission evidence. |
| Senior management | What changed for whom, material exclusion, commitments at risk, options and uncertainty. | Investment, priorities and decisions within actual authority. |
| Employees/customers and representatives | Understandable progress, remaining limitations, support/feedback routes and participation influence. | Challenge, contribute and obtain support. |
| External/regulatory audience | Verified applicable claims in the current required format. | Authorised review and submission/publication. |

## Minimum interpretation controls

For each measure give scope, period, numerator/denominator where applicable, environment, method, sample/missingness, uncertainty, baseline, actual change, evidence links and next action. Preserve adverse findings and differences between groups and contexts. Do not average unlike requirements into a universal pass percentage or treat higher satisfaction as cancelling a failed requirement.

Employee reporting includes cumulative task burden, access to critical work, support quality and meaningful resolution. Keep individual support records restricted and guard small-group disclosure. Customer reporting includes complete journeys, comprehension, recovery, channel transitions, independence and exit. Do not infer disability, diagnosis or vulnerability from behaviour.

Before collecting or reusing data, define the decision it supports, minimum necessary fields, lawful/ethical governance, access, retention and disclosure controls. Anonymous and confidential are different claims that must be substantiated. Fewer complaints can reflect an unusable reporting route; more findings can reflect better detection.

## Human and AI reporting workflow

1. Evidence owners verify collection scope and limitations.
2. AI, if authorised, assembles qualified views and flags unsupported claims, disparities and missing decisions.
3. Human reporting and subject owners review material interpretations; record dissent and uncertainty.
4. The decision owner records action, resources, receiving owner and review trigger.
5. Check the delivered report's accessibility using relevant [document/media routes](../sources/README.md#s21), then distribute within permission.
6. At the next review, verify whether action changed outcomes. Feed recurring causes into requirements, patterns, procurement, learning and investment.

Measure AI's own contribution through correction rate, verification effort, useful time released, missed findings, accessible participation and adverse effects. Report these as evidence of the evaluated workflow, not universal claims about AI.

Use the [report template](../templates/report.md). Cadence and thresholds are locally agreed; this framework sets no universal staffing, budget, maturity score or reporting frequency.

## Report system capability and actual experience

Show adoption and evidence gaps for UX and branding systems, guidance/test coverage for foundations, design/implementation/script coverage for patterns/components, affected consumers and regression work. Keep documentation completeness separate from actual tested outcomes. Report brand identification, trust, expectations, value, values and resonance within evaluated audiences/contexts alongside accessible usability and conformance. Refer to the [system process](design-and-brand-systems.md).
