# Human and AI responsibilities

Use [S02](../sources/README.md#s02) for the full human/AI RACI method and [S06](../sources/README.md#s06) for capability review. The following is a local assignment scaffold. Confirm names, deputies, authority and protected time before treating it as adopted.

## Responsibility assignment

| Activity | Proposed human accountable function | Responsible and consulted functions | AI contribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Remit, commitments and funding | Sponsor or executive owner within delegation | Programme coordinator; affected people; portfolio and policy owners | Options, dependencies and decision briefs. |
| Applicability and adopted policy | Competent legal/compliance or policy owner | Service facts owner; specialists | Source retrieval and draft applicability records. |
| Service design and procurement | Service/product owner | Procurement, architecture, UX, content, employees/customers | Briefs, evidence analysis and design alternatives. |
| Implementation and production | Delivery owner | Engineering, design, editorial and operations | Reviewable code/content and candidate fixes. |
| Evidence quality and test classification | Test/evaluation owner | QA, AT specialists, editorial reviewers, researchers and participants | Supported checks, evidence organisation and gap analysis. |
| Release or material departure | Authorised release/service owner; escalate above delegation | Relevant assurance, legal and operational reviewers | Evidence package and recommendations. |
| Operation and corrective action | Operational service owner | Support, delivery teams, suppliers | Routing, approved monitoring and follow-up. |
| Programme reporting | Programme/reporting owner | Evidence owners, privacy and leadership | Qualified summaries and claim checks. |
| AI permission and control changes | Delegated AI governance owner | Service, privacy, security and affected users | Proposals and logged execution within limits. |

Assign exactly one human A and at least one human R per adopted activity. The same person can be R and A when proportionate. Keep independent review for significant policy, evidence and AI-control decisions. A champions network supports capability and challenge; it does not inherit all service accountability.

## Choose AI involvement per task

| Level | Mode | Evidence and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A0 | Human process | All steps usable without AI; ordinary records still apply. |
| A1 | Advisory | Retrieve and draft within the task mandate; verify material claims and references. |
| A2 | Assisted execution | Authorised checks or reviewable changes in a defined environment; scope, logs, review and recovery required. |
| A3 | Delegated routine coordination | Defined low-impact operations with authenticated delegation, access controls, monitoring and stop conditions. |

A3 is not the goal for every task. Compare the useful contribution with error risk, verification burden, privacy, recoverability and available capability. Tool access and a mature programme do not grant authority.

For A2/A3, record purpose, human owner, permitted actions and targets, data access, environment, delegation reference, prohibited actions, verification, time/volume limits, stop conditions and recovery. Use the [delegation record](../templates/decision-and-evidence.md#delegation-record). The organisation's actual systems enforce permissions; these documents do not.

## Decisions retained by humans

Appropriately authorised humans adopt policy and commitments; approve spending; determine legal interpretations; make employment or other rights-affecting decisions; accept material risks within lawful authority; authorise public claims; and decide release. An agent may prepare evidence and options. A generated approval field is not an authenticated decision, and an exception cannot waive a binding obligation.

An agent can support routine approved operations without a new confirmation on each step when its mandate already covers the action. Pause affected execution when scope, data, authority or material risk exceeds that mandate. Continue independent authorised drafting and analysis.

## Make AI's benefit observable

At a pilot, compare the previous and proposed process: time released, errors found/missed, correction rate, review effort, participant outcomes and unintended effects. Preserve uncertainty and confounders. Repeated wrong citations, unreviewable outputs, privacy concerns or inaccessible interactions trigger a review of tools and delegation. Maintain an accessible human route and the ability to stop or reverse work.

## System ownership and QA alignment

Assign responsible UX design-system and brand-system owners, with engineering, content, QA and research contributions and one human A per adopted activity. They maintain guidance, reference assets, many-to-many test/consumer relationships and evidence. Programme management funds capability gaps; AI may propose links, drafts and checks but cannot validate its own usability or brand effectiveness. Use the [system process](design-and-brand-systems.md).
